Bounce Drop - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-11

Summary

Bounce Drop is a simple one-tap arcade game. We do not ask for your name, email or any personal details, and we do not run ads or analytics inside the game.

What is stored

The game stores your best score so it can show it next time you play. On websites such as itch.io this is saved in your own browser (local storage). On Facebook Instant Games it is saved with the game's player data provided by the platform. If a leaderboard is available, your score may be shown there by the platform.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell or share personal information. The platform that hosts the game (for example Facebook or itch.io) may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
