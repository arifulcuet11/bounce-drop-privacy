Bounce Drop - User Data Deletion

Bounce Drop does not collect or store personal information on our servers. The only data the game keeps is your best score, saved by the hosting platform (Facebook Instant Games) or in your browser.

How to delete your data

  • On Facebook: open Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Games), find Bounce Drop and remove it. This clears the game's saved data.
  • In a web browser: clear this site's local storage / site data.
